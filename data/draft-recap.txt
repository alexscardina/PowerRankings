Draft Recap
As we as a group get better and better at fantasy drafting, these rankings get harder and harder. Last year I said that it was definitely the hardest draft rankings I’ve had to do yet, but this year seemed harder. It would probably be far more appropriate to do a tier list, rather than ranking everyone individually, but that’s less fun and creates less engagement. I can’t wait until all of this is completely nullified after week 1, enjoy!


12. Cyborg Squad (Austin)
Strengths: QB, TE, RB Depth
Weaknesses: WR, WR Depth, stray shards of glass
Best Pick: 4.03 - Cam Skattebo
Worst Pick: 2.03 - Trey McBride

I watched my friends get swept away and stranded by a flash flood, stood in the pouring rain and freezing cold for three hours, and needed to get rescued with life jackets and ropes with the rest of us, yet seeing this team be assembled was the greatest disaster I witnessed this weekend. Bro doubled up on the Bears’ backfield and didn’t take his WR2 until round 8. Looks like my/our predictions that Austin will eat wings for the third year in a row already look pretty good.


11. Rise of the Slim Reaper (Richard)
Strengths: RB, TE
Weaknesses: WR, FLEX, an allergy/aversion to taking WRs early
Best Pick: 5.04 - Colston Loveland
Worst Pick: 6.09 - Alec Pierce

Richard usually ends up with sub-par drafts and finds his way into the playoffs anyway, so it’s likely this ranking will be complete hogwash like a month from now, but we rank rosters, not managers, and this roster is not incredibly inspiring. It should be stated that this felt low for Richard’s team, so I once again put it into a league analyzer and 113 fantasy “experts” also put his team 11th, so credit to me for checking my work. Also he called me the “weakest link” while we were getting rescued so, 🖕


10. Get these Nabers out of here (Matt)
Strengths: TE, WR
Weaknesses: TE, WR Depth, believing in the Giants
Best Pick: 4.08 - Jaylen Waddle
Worst Pick: 7.05 - Jaxson Dart

Matt forgot that Cam is the only one allowed to draft CMC and have it work out, the rest of us are doomed to fail when we take him. So, good luck with your 30-year-old high-volume running back with degenerative knees, alongside your two injury-prone New York Giants players. I’m sure that’ll work out spectacularly.


9. Strong Njigbas (Tyler)
Strengths: WR, FLEX
Weaknesses: QB, RB, not coming to Puerto Rico
Best Pick: 2.05 - CeeDee Lamb
Worst Pick: 3.08 - Quinshon Judkins

Year after year, Tyler is getting slightly better at this whole drafting thing. Sure the team isn’t perfect, and he might have reached on his two RBs both rehabbing from season-ending injuries, and he took Bo Nix a bit too early. But honestly it’s not all that bad. Two elite WRs to start off, then Williams in the 5th, then you get some upside swings with Lloyd and Likely. There is very little separating this team from some of the top teams. It’s just that Ty is pretty used to being ranked down here, so why not do it again?


8. New Year Chris Leak (Connor)
Strengths: RB, WR
Weaknesses: FLEX, TE, Cueva del Mar
Best Pick: 9.02 - Trevor Lawrence
Worst Pick: 4.11 - Davante Adams

Every year, Connor pulls up to the draft not knowing a single player or rankings, reads an article like 30 minutes before draft time, drafts virtually only off of ESPN’s rankings and projected points, and ends up doing just fine. Considering how well the Quinshon pick worked out for him last year, it stands to reason that Josh Jacobs will probably end up being a good pick too. I mean, Connor took a team of shoestring and duct tape all the way to second place last year, maybe it’s my downfall that I should dare rank him this low.


7. HE. BLEEDS. PINK. (Xander)
Strengths: RB, WR
Weaknesses: FLEX, Depth, not checking key boxes for keys
Best Pick: 5.03 - Tee Higgins
Worst Pick: 4.10 - Sam Laporta

It seems that once again the Machine favors Xander over Austin, seeing as it gave him a far superior team. It’s possible the Machine employs some sort of positive reinforcement tactics and rewards Xander for his success while punishing Austin for his failures. While Xander does not know how to check the lock box outside of the house to find the keys, he DOES know how to check the waiver wire to find some gems — which is ultimately what led to his championship last year — so maybe we should expect similar success this season.


6. team of bums v9 (Rocco)
Strengths: QB, RB, WR
Weaknesses: TE, Depth, not understanding nominative determinism
Best Pick: 13.11 - Dalton Kincaid
Worst Pick: 6.02 - Carnell Tate

While my love affair with Rocco’s team has come to its untimely end, I’m still finding reasons to lust after it. The big positive with Rocco’s team is that basically every pick is a talented player projected for high volume. The negative is that three of those top players represent the Titans, Jets, and Dolphins offenses, so maybe it’s perfectly accurate to call them all bums. Don’t be surprised when Rocco is averaging under 2 touchdowns a week after the first month.


5. Scott Hanson’s Protege (Cameron)
Strengths: WR, RB, QB
Weaknesses: Depth, TE, rocks
Best Pick: 5.12 - Drake Maye
Worst Pick: 6.01 - Marvin Harrison Jr.

In what ended up an EXTREMELY tumultuous weekend for Cam, he started it out with a draft that was pretty nice. Securing two top-end RBs early, then filling the gaps with WRs and a top-tier fantasy QB is a pretty solid strategy. The big issue is that he took a WR with no toe, he fell into both the Marv trap AND the Pitts trap AGAIN, and he took a player who rehabbed his knee in jail. So sure, there’s reason to be optimistic, and maybe out of pity I should rank him higher, but 5 feels appropriate for a team with this many red flags.


4. Brock Hard (Alex)
Strengths: WR, RB, FLEX
Weaknesses: QB, Depth, breathing
Best Pick: 5.09 - Luther Burden III
Worst Pick: 4.04 - Travis Etienne Jr. (all my picks are good fuck all of you)

It’s possible this is me being humble and polite, not ranking myself first, and it’s possible I have beer goggles on and this team should be ranked far lower, but fuck it we ball. It’s hard for me to properly criticize my team, since I got basically exactly who I wanted when I wanted them, so I’ll try making the bear case for fun; I have two players on a bottom-12 projected offense, an injury-prone RB1 on a team who has consistently underperformed, the lowest-ranked starting QB of all of us, and a bunch of high-variance bums from rounds 5-7. So I guess it’s not all rosy over here (he says, lying).


3. I Want Cam To Gibbs Me Head (Steven)
Strengths: Gibbs, WR, FLEX
Weaknesses: RB, FLEX Depth, the inevitable inconsistency and playoff bouncing this team will face
Best Pick: 3.01 - Nico Collins
Worst Pick: 7.01 - Harold Fannin Jr.

It’s hard to rank a team with Jahmyr Gibbs any lower than 3rd, and Steve followed up pick 1 with a banger 2-3 turn. The picks get less and less good with each consecutive turn (some might say that’s how drafts usually work) but there’s a really strong foundation here. With some bounces the right way, and some good injury luck, it’d be easy to see this team as a championship contender. So, we know that’s not going to happen for Steven, and by picking Gibbs he’s doomed every other Gibbs owner. Probably.


2. Jackson Off On Chase’s TDs (Nick)
Strengths: WR, FLEX, QB
Weaknesses: TE, RB, prioritizing injuries to his crotch instead of his head
Best Pick: 2.07 - Justin Jefferson
Worst Pick: 4.07 - Jadarian Price

It’s gonna be difficult to criticize this team when the two best WRs in the NFL fell to him at crazy values in the first two rounds. I guess it’s not ideal to have a rookie RB in a committee as your RB2, and I guess I’d probably not have picked Johnson as TE1? Otherwise this is a solid team. Maybe it’s fitting that I give Nick 2nd place in these rankings, since he’s so used to finishing there. Always the bridesmaid.


1. McConkeez Nuts (Liam)
Strengths: RB, WR, TE
Weaknesses: Depth, having zero rizz despite having zero body fat
Best Pick: 4.06 - Ladd McConkey
Worst Pick: 7.07 - Jonathan Brooks, I guess?

I can’t find a weakness in this team. If I squint, I could say taking a TE in the 5th and overlooking better flex depth is bad, or I could say that having limited WR depth would cripple this team. But those are really minor nitpicks; the foundation is extremely strong and picks 2-6 are all great value. Maybe this ranking is for drafting my favorite team, or maybe this is for lowkey saving my brother’s life on Sunday. In any case, Liam gets the post-draft crown. So congrats Liam, enjoy it while it lasts; you of all people should know that being number one in the power rankings means absolutely nothing!
